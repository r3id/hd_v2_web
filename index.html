Frequently asked questions

What types of property can be listed?
Flats, houses, studios, rooms in shared properties and HMOs. If it's safe, compliant and lettable, a council near you likely needs it.
What does it cost to list?
Nothing. There are no listing fees and no commission. Landlords and agents list free and keep the full agreed rent.
How are landlords and properties vetted?
Every landlord is ID- and document-checked before they can list, and each property must have current certificates. If one runs out, the listing comes off the site until a valid replacement is uploaded.
Who handles maintenance?
Occupants report and track issues online 24/7, with photo uploads and live messaging, so problems reach the right person without phone tag.
How quickly can a council get started?
Councils can be searching the same day. We'll set up your area, saved searches and team access, then walk you through your first placement.
