Local authority questions

How are landlords and properties vetted?
Every landlord is ID- and document-checked before they can list, and each property must have current certificates. You only ever see stock that meets the standard.
How quickly can we get started?
Councils can be searching the same day. We'll set up your patch, saved searches and team access, then walk you through your first placement.
Can we list council-owned stock as well?
Yes. Council-owned units are managed from the same dashboard with the same listing, booking and compliance tools, and you choose per listing whether a unit stays internal to your team or is shared with neighbouring authorities.
Does it integrate with our housing management system?
Yes. We connect with common systems including Capita, Civica, NEC Housing, Jigsaw, Microsoft 365 and GOV.UK Notify, so there is no double-entry.
Is household data secure?
Household data is stored in the UK, shared only with the matched landlord on a confirmed booking, and every access is recorded in an immutable audit log. We are Cyber Essentials certified and ISO 27001 aligned.
What does it cost?
Pricing is tailored to your placement volumes and the modules you need. Book a demo and we'll walk you through a model that fits your authority.
