About us

We're fixing how councils house people.

Every day, councils spend hours chasing landlords by phone while families wait. Housing Direct puts vetted stock, compliant paperwork and a full audit trail in one place, so placements take days, not months.

Our mission

A home for every household, without the friction

We started Housing Direct after watching housing teams fight spreadsheets and voicemail to place vulnerable families. We believed the whole thing could be faster, safer and fully accountable.

  • Compliant by default: listings can't go live without current certificates.
  • Accountable: every decision is logged to a tamper-evident trail.
  • Built with councils: shaped by the teams who use it every day.
