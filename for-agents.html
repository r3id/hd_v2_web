Agent questions

Can we list on behalf of our landlords?
Yes, that's the point. You verify your agency once and confirm your authority to act for each landlord. Their properties then sit in their own portfolio under your account.
What does it cost?
Listing is free. There are no listing fees or commission, and you keep your existing arrangement with your landlords.
What types of property can we list?
Flats, houses, studios, rooms in shared properties and HMOs. If it's safe, compliant and lettable, a council near you likely needs it.
How does compliance work across many properties?
Certificates are tracked per property with reminders before anything expires. If one lapses, that listing is automatically suspended until a valid replacement is uploaded. Nothing else is affected.
Who handles maintenance?
Occupants report and track issues through the Arthur app 24/7, with photos and live messaging, so requests reach the right portfolio without phone tag.
