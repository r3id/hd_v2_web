What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site work, remember your preferences, and understand how it is used.

How we use cookies

We use cookies to keep you signed in, remember settings, keep the platform secure, and, with your consent, measure and improve how the site performs.

Managing cookies

You can accept or reject non-essential cookies via our cookie banner, and change your choice at any time. Most browsers also let you block or delete cookies. Note that blocking essential cookies may stop parts of the platform working.

Third-party cookies

Some cookies are set by trusted third parties, for example analytics or embedded media. These providers process data under their own policies and our data processing agreements.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy as our use of cookies changes. The "last updated" date above reflects the current version.
