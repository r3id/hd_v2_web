Somewhere today, a housing officer is working through a list of households who need a home, ringing round landlords one at a time. Somewhere else, a landlord with an empty, fully compliant property is wondering why it has sat vacant for six weeks. Neither of them knows the other exists.
{: .lead-in}

That gap is the whole reason Housing Direct exists.

## The problem isn't a shortage of effort

Housing teams are not short of urgency. They are short of visibility. Finding suitable accommodation still runs on spreadsheets, personal contacts and phone calls, and the same three or four agents get called because they are the ones whose numbers people have. Every placement means re-checking certificates, re-keying details and rebuilding a paper trail from scratch.

On the other side, landlords and letting agents who would happily work with a council often have no straightforward way in. The route is different in every area, the paperwork is opaque, and the reputation of council lettings is a decade out of date.

The result is slow placements, empty compliant homes, and families waiting in accommodation nobody thinks is suitable.

## What we're building

Housing Direct is one platform where accredited landlords and letting agents list properties, and Local Authorities search, book and manage placements in the areas they need.

- **Every listing checked and inspected.** Landlords are ID and document-verified, and properties carry current certificates. Housing teams only ever see stock that meets the standard.
- **Simple booking, placement and management.** Shortlist, send a booking request with occupant details, sign digitally, and keep managing the placement afterwards, with simple invoicing.
- **A full audit trail, automatically.** Every search, request and approval is recorded as it happens, so evidencing a decision later isn't an archaeology project.
- **Free to list, with no commission.** Landlords and agents keep the full agreed rent, and get access to guaranteed rent schemes where the placing authority runs one.
