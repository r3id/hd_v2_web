For most landlords, "renting to the council" still carries an outdated reputation. But the reality in 2026 looks very different, and for a growing number of landlords, a local authority is the most reliable tenant they've ever had.
{: .lead-in}

The open market promises the highest headline rent. What it rarely mentions are the voids between tenancies, the arrears when circumstances change, and the management overhead that quietly eats into the return. When you actually net it out, a council placement often comes out ahead.</p>

## The income is reliable
With a council placement, rent is paid directly by the local authority on a fixed schedule for the length of the agreement. There's no chasing, no missed months, and no void period while the property sits empty between tenants. Many placing authorities also run guaranteed rent schemes, and listing with us gives you access to them where they are available.

{% include pull.html text="One listing now reaches every council in our area. Our voids are filled faster and the compliance side runs itself." %}

That predictability changes how you plan. A dependable monthly figure you can count on is worth more than a higher number you might not collect.

## The terms are longer
Councils are placing households who need somewhere stable to live, often for years. That means longer agreements, lower turnover, and far fewer of the re-letting costs that erode open-market returns.

## The admin is handled
This is where the reputation is most out of date. On Housing Direct, the parts landlords dread are handled for you:
- The local authority holds the tenancy and manages the household day to day.
- Your compliance certificates are tracked automatically, with reminders before anything lapses.
- Booking requests arrive with occupant details already filled in, and the contract is generated for you.


## So what's the catch?
Honestly, there isn't much of one. Your property needs to meet standard letting requirements: a valid energy certificate, gas and electrical safety, and good repair. If it does, you can be listed and placed within the week.
