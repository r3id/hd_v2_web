Questions

Council housing team questions

Can we list council-owned stock alongside private landlords?
Yes. Your council-owned units are managed from the same dashboard and use the same listing, booking and compliance tools as private stock — you simply own and control your own listings.
Can we keep some listings internal?
You choose per listing whether a unit stays internal to your team or is shared with neighbouring authorities to widen reach and fill voids faster.
How does compliance work across a large portfolio?
Gas, EPC, EICR, insurance, fire risk and asbestos are tracked per unit, with reminders before anything expires. If a certificate lapses, the affected listing is automatically suspended until a valid replacement is uploaded.
Can we import our existing stock?
Yes — we support bulk import to get your portfolio set up quickly, and our team will help with onboarding and staff access.
Is household data secure?
Household data is stored in the UK, shared only on a confirmed placement on a need-to-know basis, and every access is recorded in an immutable audit log. We're Cyber Essentials certified and ISO 27001 aligned.
