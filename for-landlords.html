Questions

Everything you need to know

Who pays the rent?
The local authority pays you directly on a fixed schedule for the full length of the placement — not the tenant. That's what makes the income reliable and void-free while your property is placed.
What does it cost to list?
Nothing. There are no listing fees and no agency commission. You keep the full agreed rent.
How quickly can my property be placed?
Councils have households waiting now, so eligible properties are often matched within the same week they're listed. The first payment typically lands within three days of a placement starting.
What condition does my property need to be in?
It needs to meet standard letting requirements — a valid energy certificate, gas and electrical safety, and to be in good repair. We track all of this for you and flag anything missing before you list.
Do I still manage the tenancy?
No — the local authority holds the tenancy and manages the household. You remain the property owner and receive the rent, without the day-to-day management.
