Landlord questions

What does it cost?
Nothing. There are no listing fees and no commission. You keep the full agreed rent.
What types of property do you accept?
Flats, houses, studios, rooms in shared properties and HMOs. If it's safe, compliant and lettable, a council near you likely needs it.
Can I access a guaranteed rent scheme?
Many placing authorities run guaranteed rent schemes, and listing with us gives you access to them where they are available. Terms are set by the authority and shown before you accept a booking.
What condition does my property need to be in?
Safe, clean and compliant: current gas, EPC, EICR and appropriate licences where required. Our vetting team reviews every listing before it goes live.
Who handles maintenance?
Occupants report and track issues online 24/7, with photo uploads and live messaging, so problems reach you without phone tag.
