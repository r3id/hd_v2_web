Landlord questions

What does it cost?
Nothing. There are no listing fees and no commission. You keep the quoted nightly rate.
What types of property do you accept?
Flats, houses, studios, rooms in shared properties and HMOs. If it's safe, compliant and lettable, a council near you likely needs it.
How does guaranteed rent work?
Placements are backed by the placing authority, so rent is paid on time for the full term of the placement.
What condition does my property need to be in?
Safe, clean and compliant: current gas, EPC, EICR and appropriate licences where required. Our vetting team reviews every listing before it goes live.
Who handles maintenance?
Occupants report and track issues through the Arthur app 24/7, with photo uploads and live messaging, so problems reach you without phone tag.
